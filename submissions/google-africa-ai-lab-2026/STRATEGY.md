## Positioning

> Abada is an open-source platform for building, running and continuously
> improving AI-powered business processes — with durable orchestration and
> human-governed change.

The narrative is one complete lifecycle:

`Create or import → Run → Observe → Improve`

- **Create** includes visual design, AI-assisted authoring, APL, human tasks and decisions.
- **Import** is one interoperability option, not the product identity.
- **Run** coordinates Gemini agents, people and enterprise systems durably.
- **Observe** persists history, failures, latency and agent-attempt metadata.
- **Improve** uses Insight findings and governed proposal adoption.

APL is the native, reviewable representation beneath Studio. BPMN import is an
adoption feature. Neither replaces the lifecycle as the headline value.

## Demo decision

The demo opens on Studio's three creation paths, then follows one created
lead-triage process through Gemini execution, durable telemetry and Insight
review. BPMN import receives one brief proof shot. APL source scrolling is not
the narrative.

If the live model or Insight state is unavailable, use clearly labeled
integration evidence. Never manufacture a model response or present demo data
as production execution.
